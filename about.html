In his own words

The Wayfarer

I am the Chief Engineer for GenAI at Commonwealth Bank, where I lead the platform engineering that lets one of Australia’s largest enterprises adopt GenAI safely and at scale: model access, governance, observability and audit-ready controls.

On the side I run Eigenstack, a deliberately small product studio for consumer AI agents. It is where I keep my hands dirty, and most of what I write about agents comes from running them in production.

Before that I was Senior Director of Digital & AI at Optus, and held engineering leadership roles at Canva, Zip Co, RecordPoint and MODRON, after earlier years at Atlassian and ThoughtWorks.

I believe the best engineering leaders stay close enough to the work to have informed opinions, and far enough away to let their teams own it.

When I’m not working, I’m probably building voice AI agents from inconvenient locations, reading strategy books, dying repeatedly to an optional boss, or trying to explain to my family what “vibe coding” means.

The Road So Far

  1. Sep 2025 – present

    Commonwealth BankCurrent

    Chief Engineer, GenAI

    • Lead GenAI platform engineering for safe, scalable adoption across the bank: model access, risk and governance, observability and audit-ready controls.
    • Stood up lighthouse use cases that set the technical strategy for reliable AI voice agents on realtime speech-to-speech models.
    • Lead the work on agentic memory for hyperpersonalisation: extraction pipelines and retrieval efficiency.
    • Guide teams across the GenAI domain, from the agentic marketplace design and MCP tool versioning semantics to reliability in multi-agent systems.

  2. Feb 2026 – present

    EigenstackCurrent

    Founder & Builder · Product studio for consumer AI agents

    • Built and launched Hakken, an adaptive learning platform where an agent interviews the learner, generates a bespoke course and adapts it as they go.
    • Built and operated Liv4All, a multi-tenant platform of persistent personal AI agents on Telegram and WhatsApp. Around 100 agents in production before retirement.
    • Outputs include an arXiv paper and coatcheck, an open-source credential-broker sidecar extracted from the platform.

  3. Jul 2024 – Aug 2025

    Optus

    Senior Director, Digital & AI

    • Led 8 squads (around 70 engineers, data scientists and PMs) building AI for contact-centre automation, knowledge search and developer productivity.
    • Shipped “Optus Buddy”, a RAG-powered answer engine that placed 2nd of 50 teams in the company AI hackathon and won C-suite sponsorship for rollout.
    • Defined the “AI Engineer” role profile, hiring framework and upskilling programme; established the Responsible AI forum and model-governance lifecycle.

  4. Jul 2022 – Jul 2024

    Canva

    Director of Engineering, Marketing

    • Led 50+ engineers and data scientists across Martech and Digital Marketing, supporting over 500 marketers.
    • Introduced AI-driven ad-copy and image creation models; delivered flagship digital experiences including Canva Create.
    • Lifted female senior engineering leadership representation by 25%.

  5. Aug 2020 – Jul 2022

    Zip Co

    Director of Engineering → Acting CTO

    • Oversaw consumer product, mobile, rewards and quality engineering: 100+ engineers across Sydney and Ukraine.
    • Cut the mobile release cycle from fortnightly to weekly through CI/CD and feature flags.

  6. Jun 2019 – Aug 2020

    RecordPoint

    • Migrated a stateful Service Fabric legacy platform to stateless containers with zero-downtime deployments.
