The Steel Thread

A steel thread is the thinnest end-to-end path through a system that proves the architecture actually works. That is the idea behind the newsletter: trace the minimal line from idea to implementation, from claim to evidence, rather than admire the diagram.

A newsletter on engineering leadership, AI adoption and careers for senior technology leaders. Formerly The LeadTech Diet: same writer, same subscription, sharper name.
