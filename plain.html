About

I am the Chief Engineer for GenAI at Commonwealth Bank, where I lead the platform engineering that lets one of Australia’s largest enterprises adopt GenAI safely and at scale: model access, governance, observability and audit-ready controls.

On the side I run Eigenstack, a deliberately small product studio for consumer AI agents. It is where I keep my hands dirty, and most of what I write about agents comes from running them in production.

Before that I was Senior Director of Digital & AI at Optus, and held engineering leadership roles at Canva, Zip Co, RecordPoint and MODRON, after earlier years at Atlassian and ThoughtWorks.

I believe the best engineering leaders stay close enough to the work to have informed opinions, and far enough away to let their teams own it.

When I’m not working, I’m probably building voice AI agents from inconvenient locations, reading strategy books, dying repeatedly to an optional boss, or trying to explain to my family what “vibe coding” means.

Writing

The Steel Thread (formerly The LeadTech Diet) has run since 2021. A steel thread is the thinnest end-to-end path through a system that proves the architecture actually works. That is the idea behind the newsletter: trace the minimal line from idea to implementation, from claim to evidence, rather than admire the diagram.
