Eigenstack

A small studio. A few good products.

Eigenstack is my independent software studio. It designs, builds and runs consumer AI products end to end.

Small by design

No headcount goals, no growth theatre. Senior by default, shipping products end to end: design, engineering and operations.

Products we use ourselves

Everything starts as something we wanted to exist. If it wouldn’t stay on our own home screen, it doesn’t ship.

Long-term by default

Products run for years, not quarters. When one has run its course, it is wound down properly rather than left to rot.

Hakken

Legendary Catalyst · Adaptive learning platform

Live · web, iOS & Android

Learning, built around you.

Hakken interviews you about what you want to learn (your goals, your level, your schedule), generates a course tailored to you and adapts it as you go: in-lesson tutoring, agent-graded exercises, checkpoints and a voice interview that actually listens.

Named for the Japanese 発見, “discovery”. Said to listen before it teaches.

Live

The mortgage calculator that’s on your side.

Liv

Retired Companion · Personal AI agent platform

Retired · Feb – Oct 2026

An AI chief of staff, on Telegram.

A persistent AI agent that acted on your behalf: managing calendars, triaging email, making bookings. Each user had an isolated agent container on AWS. Invite-only, around 100 agents in production before it was retired. Much of what it taught went straight into Hakken.

She was a tool. Her maker kept telling himself so.
